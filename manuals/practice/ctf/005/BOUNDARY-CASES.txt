For wide-length, the two ADD instructions operate in 32 bits: keep the low
32 bits after each operation. CMP/SETBE uses UNSIGNED ordering.
The fixed predicate checks cursor <= total BEFORE subtracting, and at least
two remaining bytes BEFORE subtracting the header. Identify why both orderings
matter. A subtraction is not automatically safe merely because it is subtraction.

The wide-length vector is for the generic u32 guard API. This fixture's u8 TLV
length cannot represent it. Keep reachability and local arithmetic separate.
